# Privacy Policy for Rummy Score App

**Last updated:** May 6, 2026

## Introduction

Rummy Score ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally
Rummy Score stores all data locally on your device, including:
- Player names you create
- Game scores and history
- App preferences and settings

**We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on our servers.**

## Data Storage

All app data is stored locally on your device using secure storage mechanisms provided by the operating system. This data remains on your device and is not accessible to us.

## Third-Party Services

### Google AdMob (Advertising)
Our app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data for personalized advertising purposes. This may include:
- Device identifiers (Advertising ID)
- IP address
- General location data
- App usage data

For more information about how Google collects and uses data, please visit:
- [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses data when you use our partners' apps](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

You can opt out of personalized advertising by:
- Adjusting your device's advertising settings
- Using Google's Ads Settings at https://adssettings.google.com

### Google Play In-App Purchases
Our app offers an option to remove ads through an in-app purchase processed by Google Play. Google handles all payment information and transactions. We do not have access to your payment details.

### No Other Third-Party Services
- No analytics services
- No crash reporting services
- No cloud storage

## Data Sharing

We do not directly share any data with third parties. However, Google AdMob may share data as described in their privacy policy.

## Data Security

Since app data is stored locally on your device:
- Your data is protected by your device's security features
- Uninstalling the app will delete all locally stored data
- We cannot access, recover, or restore your local data

## Children's Privacy

Our app is designed for a general audience. While we do not directly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, please note that Google AdMob may collect certain data as described above. 

We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and similar regulations. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the advertising services, please contact Google directly.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected with a new "Last updated" date.
